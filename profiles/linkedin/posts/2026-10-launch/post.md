# Launch post: the new CV and site

Post as an image post with the four screenshots in order. Paste the text below as written; the line breaks are
deliberate, because LinkedIn shows the first three lines before "see more".

## Text

    I've rebuilt how I present my work, starting with the CV.

    Older versions of my CV were each tailored to one kind of role. This one is the whole picture: web, mobile and cloud, fourteen years of it, in one place. It lives at codesthings.com/cv as a page and as a PDF that prints properly on paper.

    The site around it got the same care:

    • A light theme like warm paper and a dark one like moonlight, with a small sundial as the mark.
    • Motion slow enough that you only notice it when it's gone.
    • Laid out for a phone from the start, not squeezed down to fit one.
    • Every change is built and checked on its own branch before it goes near the live site, and a check runs every morning to make sure it stays that way.

    The CampSnap Pro filters I made have their own page too, at codesthings.com/campsnap.

    I'm open to senior or principal remote roles. If you're hiring, or know someone who is, the CV is the place to start: codesthings.com/cv
